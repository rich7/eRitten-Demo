# About PunchPass (Rittenkaart)

**PunchPass** (published under [rich7/rittenkaart](https://github.com/rich7/rittenkaart)) is an open-source, automated **QR code punch pass & activity tracking app** designed specifically for clubs, non-profit organizations, and community associations.

It eliminates manual paper stamp cards, token counting, and tedious administrative tracking by giving members digital punch passes that can be validated in seconds using a simple QR code scan.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

* 📱 **Instant QR Code Scanning:** Scan a member's QR code on any device to validate a session or ride immediately.
* ⚡ **Automatic Balance Updates:** Credits, rides, or session balances update in real-time with zero manual bookkeeping.
* 🛡️ **Privacy-First & Self-Hosted:** Open-source architecture ensures full control over your organization's and members' data.
* 👥 **Member & Pass Management:** Easily issue new passes, review attendance history, and adjust balances.
* 💡 **Lightweight & Versatile:** Tailored for community transport, local sports clubs, workshops, and recurring group events.

---

## 🎯 Use Cases

* **Community & Shuttle Transport:** Track multi-ride vehicle passes (*rittenkaarten / beurtenkaarten*) automatically upon boarding.
* **Sports & Fitness Clubs:** Manage multi-session passes for classes, swimming sessions, or court rentals.
* **Workshops & Cultural Associations:** Track attendance and multi-entry activity cards without extra hardware.
